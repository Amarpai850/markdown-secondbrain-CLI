# Personal Knowledge Graphs

A personal knowledge graph treats every note as a node and every reference between notes as a directed edge. Linking ideas explicitly turns a pile of files into a network you can navigate.

## Backlinks and Discovery

Outgoing links record what a note depends on. Backlinks show which notes depend on it, and they often surface connections the author forgot. The [[zettelkasten-method]] relies on this kind of dense linking between small atomic notes.

## Finding Structure

Graph algorithms reveal structure in a note collection. Breadth-first search from [[graph-traversal-algorithms]] finds notes within a few hops of a topic. Degree counts identify hub notes, and nodes with no edges at all are orphans that need to be connected.

## Building a Second Brain

A knowledge graph is the backbone of the [[second-brain-methodology]]: notes that are linked get revisited, refined, and reused, much like [[evergreen-notes]].
