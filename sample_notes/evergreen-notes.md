# Evergreen Note-Taking Principles

Evergreen notes are written and organized to accumulate, develop, and evolve over years, coined by Andy Matuschak.

## Principles

- **Concept-Oriented**: Notes should be titled after the concept rather than the book or author.
- **Densely Linked**: Notes gain value primarily through links to related thoughts.
- **Revision Over Storage**: Continuously rewrite and clarify notes as understanding deepens.

Synthesizes foundational practices from the [[zettelkasten-method]] and informs digital storage strategies in [[second-brain-methodology]].
