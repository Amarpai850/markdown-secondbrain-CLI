# Productivity Heuristics and Workflows

Sustained intellectual productivity requires deliberate cognitive routines and work partitioning.

## Practical Heuristics

- **Time-Boxing**: Allocating fixed calendar intervals for dedicated deep work sessions.
- **The 2-Minute Rule**: Executing immediate tasks taking under two minutes to reduce mental overhead.
- **Energy-Matching**: Scheduling analytical and creative tasks during peak chronotype windows.
